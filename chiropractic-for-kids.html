Why It Matters

Protecting a child's body
by protecting their nerves.

Have you considered chiropractic for your kids in Ephrata PA? At Family Tree Chiropractic we firmly believe in protecting a child's body by making sure they have the best possible functioning nervous system. It's well known that children bounce back quickly — but that's related to how they feel. Physically and structurally, that isn't always the case. Children don't always verbalize exactly what they feel when they're sore, uncomfortable, or in pain. Just like with adults, if the underlying cause is left alone for too long, it grows into a bigger problem. Chiropractic care for children can be very beneficial.

Starting from the time a child is conceived, their bodies are growing and being put under stress. While the child is growing, they're usually curled up in a ball — and then they're pushed and/or pulled during birth. Take a second and think about how you would feel if today you underwent that kind of experience. All of this stress and trauma affects the spine, which can lead to issues with the nervous system. That's a problem, because the nervous system is what controls everything our body does: blinking, breathing, heartbeat, the ability to fight illness, walking, talking, thinking, speaking — everything.

Pediatric Chiropractic in Ephrata PA

Childhood doesn't slow down.
Neither does the wear on their body.

Chiropractic care can be helpful for your child's development. After birth, the physical stress and trauma continue when children grow into toddlers. They're faced with the developmental challenge of crawling and walking. While learning to overcome these challenges, there's a significant amount of straining, twisting, and falling. As growth continues and the child starts to play with pets, friends, and family, they can again endure more physical stress and trauma. Generally, children in the preschool through middle-school age range play hard and try to play without limits.

As toddlers grow into kids, they often start to develop bad posture, play contact sports, wear heavier backpacks (most likely the wrong way), and deal with the stress of more daily responsibilities. As these kids become teenagers and high-school graduates, they often join the workforce — usually jobs that require a lot of moving around and physical labor, like servers, factory workers, and the like.

At Family Tree Chiropractic, we make a point to make sure that all the falls, stress, trauma, and physical demands that your children endure don't stop them from being kids. We love treating children of all ages.

As a parent, you want to protect your child from pain and sickness as much as you can. We get that. Let us help you. Chiropractic care is all about protecting your body from harm. Our exams are extensive — so even if your child is too young to communicate, or too shy, we'll make sure we know exactly what's going on before proceeding with treatment. And we'll make sure you know what's going on every step of the way.
