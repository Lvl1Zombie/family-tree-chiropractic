What Is Spinal Decompression

Relief Without Surgery

Non-surgical spinal decompression is a motorized traction therapy that gently stretches the spine in controlled, computerized cycles. Unlike simple traction, decompression uses alternating patterns of stretch and relaxation — customized to your condition — that create negative pressure within the disc. This negative pressure causes bulging or herniated disc material to retract, draws in oxygen, water, and nutrients to rehydrate the disc, and reduces nerve compression and inflammation. The result is structural healing at the disc level, not just symptom management.

Your Treatment

Comfortable, Controlled,
Effective.

You'll be fitted with a harness around your pelvis and positioned on a computerized decompression table. The table performs gentle, rhythmic traction cycles calibrated precisely to your condition. Sessions last 20 to 45 minutes, and most patients find the treatment comfortable and relaxing.

A typical course involves 15 to 25 sessions over 4 to 6 weeks. Decompression is often combined with chiropractic adjustments, therapeutic exercise, and cold laser therapy for comprehensive results.
