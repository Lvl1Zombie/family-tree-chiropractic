What Is Cold Laser Therapy

Light That Heals

Cold laser therapy — also known as Low-Level Laser Therapy (LLLT) or photobiomodulation — uses specific wavelengths of red and near-infrared light (600–1000 nm) to interact with your tissue at the cellular level. Unlike surgical lasers, cold laser does not cut or heat tissue. Instead, photons from the laser penetrate your skin and are absorbed by the mitochondria in your cells, triggering increased ATP production, reduced oxidative stress, and accelerated tissue repair. The result is less inflammation, less pain, and faster healing — all without drugs or downtime.
