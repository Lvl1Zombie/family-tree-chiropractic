Why We Exist

We see patients every week
who've already tried everything.

You've already done the obvious. The ibuprofen. The new mattress. The PT you "graduated" from but never really solved. Maybe a doctor who said it was stress, posture, or age — and to try yoga.

Here's what almost no one explains: pain isn't the actual problem. It's a signal. The real issue — almost always — is interference somewhere in the nervous system. Spinal misalignment. Damaged peripheral nerves. Inflammation in the wrong places. Mask the signal with a pill, and the underlying cause keeps quietly getting worse.

Family Tree Chiropractic was built to treat the cause, not the noise. Drug-free. Non-surgical. Personalized to one body — yours.

"Live with it" isn't a treatment plan. It's the absence of one.
