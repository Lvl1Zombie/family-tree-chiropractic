What Is Chiropractic Care

The Foundation of
Whole-Body Wellness

Chiropractic care is a licensed healthcare discipline focused on diagnosing and treating mechanical disorders of the neuromusculoskeletal system — your nerves, muscles, joints, and spine. At its core is a powerful principle: your body has an innate ability to heal itself when your nervous system is functioning without interference.

We use hands-on spinal adjustments combined with complementary therapies to restore alignment, relieve pain, and optimize your body's natural healing capacity — all without drugs or surgery.

Our Techniques

Precision Care,
Personalized to You.

Every patient is different, and every treatment plan reflects that. Our doctors are trained in multiple adjustment techniques — including Diversified, Thompson Drop Table, Activator Method, Flexion-Distraction, and Gonstead — so we can match the right approach to your body, your condition, and your comfort level.

Whether you prefer traditional hands-on adjustments or gentler instrument-assisted techniques, we meet you where you are.
