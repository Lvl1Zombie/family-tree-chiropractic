Mission log / Experience 05

Orbital Kitchen

A playful spatial tasting menu in which plated desserts become planets and fine dining escapes its usual visual seriousness.

IdentityPlayful / spatial / precise
PaletteDeep navy, apricot, pistachio
Primary behaviorRotate and explore

Concept & process

The concept combines two systems with strong visual rules—planetary diagrams and tasting menus. The experience moves from a hero “launch,” through horizontally orbiting courses, into a mission philosophy and oversized reservation finale.

Technique & implementation

A Codex-generated dessert planet supplies the centerpiece. CSS perspective, Pointer Events, and custom properties produce drag rotation without a 3D runtime. Ingredient moons float on independent orbits; course planets use hand-built radial material gradients; the menu scrolls with snap alignment.

Three passes

01 / Composition & art direction

Built an edible solar system

  • Separated editorial copy and planet into a clean mission-control split composition.
  • Added a radial orbital halo, numbered ingredient moons, and a rotation legend.
  • Designed four distinct procedural course planets and a curved card silhouette.
02 / Motion & interaction

Added playful gravity

  • Built direct-manipulation planet rotation with bounded vertical tilt.
  • Added floating moons, parallax stars, infinite marquee motion, and staggered spatial reveals.
  • Added arrow-key rotation and a responsive reservation confirmation.
03 / Polish & technical quality

Kept the universe contained

  • Corrected transformed marquee overflow at desktop and mobile widths.
  • Added touch-action controls, keyboard focus, 44px targets, and reduced-motion fallbacks.
  • Verified snapping cards, planet drag, route links, imagery, and all mobile breakpoints.

Tools

Codex image generation, semantic HTML, modern CSS perspective and gradients, Pointer Events, IntersectionObserver, and vanilla JavaScript. The spatial interaction avoids WebGL weight while retaining tactile depth.
