Concept & process

The concept began with one tension: clinical precision versus organic wonder. The page uses tiny taxonomic labels and measurement systems around enormous, soft biological forms. The journey moves from encounter, to manifesto, to close inspection, to field records.

Technique & implementation

An original Codex-generated glass-flower image anchors the art direction. Vanilla canvas renders a low-cost spore field; pointer position creates optical parallax; a sticky 260vh anatomy sequence scales the specimen with scroll; accessible markers swap scientific notes and intensity values.

Three passes

01 / Composition & art direction

Built the specimen hierarchy

  • Strengthened the cinematic left-to-right composition around the original image.
  • Added a ten-pixel museum frame and amber archive folio to make the interface feel collected, not decorated.
  • Balanced scientific monospace metadata against the oversized humanist headline.
02 / Motion & interaction

Made observation feel alive

  • Added depth-sensitive pointer parallax, slow orbital lines, photon spores, and scroll-linked specimen magnification.
  • Introduced a quiet vertical reveal system and tactile pulsing annotation targets.
  • Made all three markers update meaningful anatomy content and measurement state.
03 / Polish & technical quality

Protected the experience at every size

  • Recomposed the hero and inspection sequence for one-column mobile layouts.
  • Expanded annotation and navigation targets, added visible focus states, and validated semantic landmark order.
  • Disabled decorative motion for reduced-motion users and verified zero horizontal overflow or broken imagery.

Tools

Codex built-in image generation created the original hero asset. HTML, modern CSS, Canvas 2D, IntersectionObserver, and requestAnimationFrame power the final local experience. No paid runtime or external deployment is required.
