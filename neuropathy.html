Understanding Neuropathy

What Is Peripheral Neuropathy?

Peripheral neuropathy is damage to the nerves outside your brain and spinal cord. These peripheral nerves carry signals between your central nervous system and the rest of your body. When damaged, they send incorrect or disrupted signals — producing pain, numbness, and dysfunction that can impact every part of your daily life.

Common Symptoms

Signs You May Have Neuropathy

Peripheral neuropathy manifests in many ways. If you experience any of the following, you may be a candidate for our treatment program.

Numbness or tingling in hands and feet
Burning, stabbing, or shooting pain
Extreme sensitivity to touch
Muscle weakness and loss of coordination
Difficulty with balance and increased fall risk
Sensation of wearing gloves or socks when you're not
Digestive or bladder problems
Dizziness or lightheadedness
