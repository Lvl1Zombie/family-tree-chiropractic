Understanding Plantar Fasciitis

Why Your Heels Hurt

Plantar fasciitis is inflammation of the plantar fascia — a thick band of tissue running along the bottom of your foot from the heel bone to the toes. It is one of the most common causes of heel pain, affecting millions of people each year. The condition develops when repetitive stress creates microtears in the fascia, leading to inflammation, stiffness, and sharp pain — especially with your first steps in the morning or after prolonged sitting.

Common Symptoms

Signs of Plantar Fasciitis

If you experience any of the following symptoms, plantar fasciitis may be the cause — and we can help.

Sharp, stabbing pain in the heel — worst in the morning
Pain along the arch of the foot
Discomfort that worsens after prolonged standing or activity
Stiffness and tenderness at the bottom of the foot
Pain that returns after long periods of rest
Difficulty walking or exercising without discomfort
