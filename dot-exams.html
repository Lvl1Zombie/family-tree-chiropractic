DOT Physical Exams

Stay Certified.
Stay on the Road.

A DOT physical exam is a federally mandated medical examination required to obtain or maintain a Commercial Driver's License (CDL). The exam certifies that you are physically qualified to safely operate a commercial motor vehicle under FMCSA regulations. Your Medical Examiner's Certificate is valid for up to 24 months, though shorter periods may be issued when monitoring specific health conditions. At Family Tree Chiropractic, our certified medical examiner provides thorough, efficient DOT physicals so you can get back on the road with confidence.

What to Expect

What the Exam Includes

Your DOT physical is a comprehensive evaluation covering every system relevant to safe commercial driving.

Vision testing: 20/40 acuity in each eye, peripheral vision, and color recognition
Hearing evaluation: forced whisper test at 5 feet
Blood pressure and cardiovascular assessment
Lung and respiratory function screening
Neurological examination and reflex testing
Musculoskeletal assessment: spine, joints, strength, and flexibility
Urinalysis: screening for diabetes, kidney, and metabolic conditions
Complete medical history and medication review
How to Prepare

Walk In Ready,
Walk Out Certified.

Getting the most out of your DOT physical starts with preparation. Complete your medical history form accurately and honestly before your appointment. Bring documentation of any current medications, including dosage and prescribing physician. If you use eyeglasses or hearing aids, bring them.

Have records of any chronic condition management — especially blood pressure, diabetes, or sleep apnea documentation. If you have concerns about meeting specific requirements, we can discuss them before your exam and help you prepare.
