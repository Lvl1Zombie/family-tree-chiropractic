Accident Injury Care

Don't Wait.
Heal Now.

Auto accidents produce sudden, violent forces that your body was never designed to absorb. Even low-speed collisions can cause significant structural damage to your spine, muscles, ligaments, and nerves — damage that may not produce symptoms for 24 to 72 hours or even days after the impact. Delaying treatment allows scar tissue to form, restricts mobility, and dramatically increases your risk of developing chronic pain. The earlier you begin care, the better your outcome. At Family Tree Chiropractic, we specialize in diagnosing and treating the full range of auto accident injuries.

Common Injuries

What Auto Accidents
Do to Your Body

Collision forces cause a wide range of injuries that often go undetected without a proper examination.

Whiplash: neck muscle, ligament, and disc damage from rapid back-and-forth motion
Soft tissue injuries: sprains and strains throughout the neck, back, and shoulders
Herniated or bulging discs from impact compression
Spinal misalignment and vertebral subluxation
Headaches, dizziness, and cognitive symptoms from cervical trauma
Radiating pain, numbness, or tingling from nerve compression
Comprehensive Treatment

Structural Correction,
Not Just Pain Management.

Our accident injury treatment goes beyond temporary relief. We use chiropractic adjustments to correct spinal misalignments caused by impact, soft tissue therapy to break down adhesions and restore flexibility, spinal decompression for disc injuries, cold laser therapy to reduce inflammation and promote tissue repair, and rehabilitative exercises to rebuild strength and stability.

We create a personalized treatment plan based on your specific injuries, and we assist with documentation for insurance claims and personal injury cases. Most auto insurance policies (PIP/MedPay) cover chiropractic care following an accident.
